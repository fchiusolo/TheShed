Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

Lumen respects your privacy. This app is designed to work fully on-device and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data Collection

Lumen stores your telescope profile exclusively on your device. No data is transmitted to any external server or third party.

Accounts & Tracking

Lumen does not require user registration, does not implement analytics, and does not include third-party tracking software or SDKs.

Cloud Sync

There is no cloud sync in Lumen. All data remains on your device at all times. No backup or syncing service is connected.
