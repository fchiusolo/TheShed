Moonbound

An educational AR companion for lunar observation

What it is

Moonbound turns a telescope-mounted iPhone into a live guide to the Moon. Point it at the eyepiece and it recognizes lunar features in real time, overlaying names and context on top of what you're actually seeing.

  • • Nightly feature recommendations based on the current moon phase
  • • Live AR overlays that identify lunar maria in real time as you observe
  • • Guided, one-target-at-a-time discovery, with a quick-info card for each feature you find
  • • Automatic observation journal — every feature you find is logged for you, with a detail page for facts like diameter and age

Why it exists

As a beginner, it's hard to know what you're actually looking at when you point a telescope at the Moon — craters and maria blur together without context. It's built as a daily observation ritual for beginner astronomers.

Availability

Moonbound is currently in development. It will be available on the App Store for iPhone.
